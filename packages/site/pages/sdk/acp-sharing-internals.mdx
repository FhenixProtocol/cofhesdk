# Share registry internals

This page is for readers who write their own registry client or label encoder, or want to know what [sharing on-chain](/sdk/acp#sharing-on-chain) costs. Using shares and labels through the SDK needs none of it.

## How the registry keeps a share

The registry keeps in storage only what it checks itself — the share **header**: issuer, expiration, recipient, revoker, and the block of the share's `Shared` event. The full payload and its metadata travel in that event:

```solidity
event Shared(address indexed recipient, address indexed issuer, bytes32 indexed shareId, ACP acp, bytes metadata);
```

`getIncomingShares` reads the headers with `sharesFor` (one call, filtered for expiry and revocation on-chain), then the events with one single-block `getLogs` per block, filtered by the share ids — a query any node answers, however old the block. If a share was removed and posted again within one block, that block holds two of its events; the later one is the share. Contracts keep `isShareValid(shareId)`; they cannot read a share's handles. Removing a share deletes its header; its event stays in its block, and readers no longer find it.

## Gas

Gas of `share(acp, metadata)`, measured on Arbitrum Sepolia with ACPs as the SDK creates them (default revoker and creation timestamp set). L2 execution gas; the L1 data part depends on the calldata.

| Share        | Without labels | With labels            |
| ------------ | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1 ctHash     | 186k           | 187k (36-byte blob)    |
| 7 ctHashes   | 191k           | 194k (122-byte blob)   |
| 20 ctHashes  | 201k           | 207k (288-byte blob)   |
| 100 ctHashes | 265k           | 291k (1,248-byte blob) |

A recipient's first share costs about 17k more (it starts their list).

## How a label is checked

- A **stored value** is checked by repeating the view call at the labelled block: the returned word must be the ctHash. Once no node keeps the state of that block, the check returns `unverifiable` (an archive node is needed for old blocks).
- An **event value** is checked against the log at the labelled block and log index: it must come from the labelled contract and transaction, its topic0 must start with the labelled selector, and the ctHash must be one of its topics or data words. Free nodes answer single-block log queries for blocks years old.

Requests are shared across the labels of one share: one `getLogs` per contract and block however many values it carries, and one call per distinct stored read.

## Layout

One blob per share, version `0x03`. Contracts, selectors and transactions are listed once; each entry points to them by position. Entries follow the order of the share's handles. Integers are big-endian; sizes in bytes.

```text
blob = version (1)
     | contracts:     count (1), 20 bytes each
     | selectors:     count (1), 4 bytes each      function selectors, and event selectors (first 4 bytes of topic0)
     | transactions:  count (1), 6 bytes each      block (4), tx index (2)
     | entries: one per handle, in the share's order

stored value = 0x00 | contract (1) | selector (1) | block (4) | args                    8 bytes or more
event value  = 0x01 | contract (1) | transaction (1) | log index (2) | selector (1)     6 bytes
unlabelled   = 0xff                                                                     1 byte

args     = 0x00                                 no arguments
         | 0x01                                 one argument: the share issuer
         | 0xff | return word (1) | count (1) | argument, count times
argument = 0x00 | word (32)                     a literal ABI word
         | 0x01                                 the share issuer
```

A real share on Arbitrum Sepolia — two balances and five transfer amounts from four transactions — is 122 bytes:

```text
03                                           version 3
02                                           2 contracts
   389be1eb842290f264a6851f49c0149b8035a052     [0] fUSD
   06a52cf2e0ceafc7d1f522400500ca7f3df18754     [1] fUSDy
02                                           2 selectors
   344ff101                                     [0] confidentialBalanceOf(address)
   67500e8d                                     [1] ConfidentialTransfer(address,address,bytes32)
04                                           4 transactions
   12ba9304 0005                                [0] block 314217220, tx 5
   12ba9307 0008                                [1] block 314217223, tx 8
   12ba9309 000a                                [2] block 314217225, tx 10
   12ba9375 000c                                [3] block 314217333, tx 12

00 00 00 12bb9d0f 01      stored value: fUSD, confidentialBalanceOf(issuer) at block 314285327
00 01 00 12bb9d0f 01      stored value: fUSDy, confidentialBalanceOf(issuer) at block 314285327
01 00 00 001e 01          event value: fUSD, transaction 0, log 30, ConfidentialTransfer
01 00 01 0041 01          event value: fUSD, transaction 1, log 65, ConfidentialTransfer
01 00 02 008a 01          event value: fUSD, transaction 2, log 138, ConfidentialTransfer
01 00 03 006a 01          event value: fUSD, transaction 3, log 106, ConfidentialTransfer
01 01 03 0073 01          event value: fUSDy, transaction 3, log 115, ConfidentialTransfer
```

The last two are the two values of one vault deposit and share its transaction. The fUSDy amount appears in two logs of that transaction (115 and 118); the label names the `ConfidentialTransfer`, and a check against log 118 fails on its selector.

**Limits.** 255 contracts, selectors and transactions per share (one-byte counts and positions); blocks up to 4.29 billion; 65,535 transactions per block and logs per block; stored-value arguments are static 32-byte words only.

**Disclosure.** A label names one log. Where a ctHash appears in several logs, the label points at the one the issuer chose; the recipient learns that log's other parameters too.

The format speaks of contracts, selectors and positions in the chain only; it knows nothing of tokens. `encodeShareMetadata` and `decodeShareMetadata` in `@cofhe/sdk/acps` read and write it.
