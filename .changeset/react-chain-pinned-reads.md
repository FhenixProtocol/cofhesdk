Chain-pinned reads: `useCofheReadContract`, `useCofheReadContracts` and `useCofheReadContractAndDecrypt` accept `chainId` — the read's chain, and its cache key's chain segment. On its own it GUARDS the read: it runs only while the wallet is connected to that chain, and otherwise stays disabled with the new `disabledDueToWrongChain` flag — never a silent read of the wrong chain. Pass a `publicClient` with it to serve the read through that client instead, wherever the wallet sits (or with no wallet connected); a client whose own chain disagrees with `chainId` keeps the read disabled. ACP gating and decryption follow the read's chain: `useCofheActiveACP(chainId?)` looks up that chain's ACP (the connected chain by default), and the decrypt step runs against it. A write's block-aware invalidation applies only to targets on the chain it was mined on: a descriptor's chain (its `chainId`, else the connected chain), a cofhe key's chain segment, or the new optional `targetChainId` on filters targets. Targets on another chain — or whose chain the hook cannot tell — get a plain refresh, never a watermark for a block their node will not see; a cofhe prefix that spans every chain (no chain segment) is refreshed everywhere and gated on the mined chain's slice only.
